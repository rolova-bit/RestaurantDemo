🍕 Pizza

Enter the number of pizzas sold by size. For the 3 special pizzas, use the special row.

Special: Mimosa Over Loaded, Sausage Pepperoni Mesh, Pepperoni Jalapeno Rush.

Automatic calculation: Chicken is 60 g / 90 g / 120 g per small / medium / large pizza. Mozzarella is 50 g / 80 g / 110 g for normal pizzas and 80 g / 120 g / 160 g for special pizzas.

🧀🍗 Pizza Extras

Enter only the pizzas that use these specific ingredients. Cheddar Heaven also uses the normal pizza chicken and mozzarella amounts; this section adds the cheddar slices separately.

Cheddar Heaven

Cheddar slices: Small 5 · Medium 5 · Large 7. Cheddar is counted in pieces, not grams.

Chicken Balls

Chicken balls are counted in pieces: Chicken Pizza = 4 / 4 / 7 and Mimosa Over Loaded = 4 / 4 / 7 for Small / Medium / Large.

Sausages

Sausages: Sausage Garden = 5 each size; Mimosa, Sausage Pepperoni Mesh, Pepperoni Jalapeno Rush = 2 each size.

🧀 Extra toppings

Enter the quantity for each size. Totals are calculated in grams.

🍟 Fries & Wedges

120 g raw per serving.

🍗 Wings

Count pieces, not grams.

5-piece orders count as 5 wings; 10-piece orders count as 10.

🥤 Shakes

Ice-cream shakes: Strawberry, Oreo, Butterscotch, Hazelnut.

Every shake uses normal milk powder + raw milk: small 15 g + 1 glass; regular 30 g + 2 glasses. Ice-cream amount is 80 g small / 120 g regular. Chocolate Coffee has no ice cream and uses 3 g / 6 g coffee.

🧋 Boba Bubble

Boba Supreme is not available yet.

Every Boba item: 120 g corresponding ice cream + 15 g milk powder + 1 glass raw milk.

🥤 Soft Drinks

Mineral Water is counted by bottles. Coke and Sprite are combined into one glass total.

250 ml = 1 glass · 350 ml = 1.5 glasses. Coke + Sprite are combined.

🍗 Chicken Delight

Enter the sold order/quantity counts. Where the menu has multiple portion sizes, select the corresponding order size.

The supplied rules specify these piece sizes but do not give gram amounts.

🦑 Fish & Seafood

Squid Fry = 6 squid pieces per quantity. Each Fish & Chips order = 1 whole Dori fish + 120 g raw fries. Prawn Tempura is unavailable.
